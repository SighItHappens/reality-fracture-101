The Five Colleges

Each college is a two-color pair with a clear theme. Your pool usually points at one of these.

Fatehold

Flyers + scry/surveil payoffs

  • Denzilore FateholdBomb

    The namesake Elder Sphinx — flash flier that puts a +1/+1 counter on your whole team every time you scry or surveil. If you open it, WU is locked in.

  • Prudent FateseerEnabler

    Fuels the scry/surveil plan while picking up value.

  • Diviner of VictoryPayoff

    Rewards you for the card selection you're already doing.

  • Surveillance PhantasmPayoff

    Evasive flier that scales with your card selection.

  • Fatehold CharmUtility

    Flexible modes — patches gaps in a sealed pool.

  • Mindseeker OculusEnabler

    Card advantage that also mills you — a natural bridge into UB graveyard decks.

Theorix

Self-mill → threshold

  • Theorix MetamagePayoff

    Namesake legend and the heart of the mill plan.

  • Void ExtrapolatorEnabler

    Pushes cards into the graveyard and profits from it.

  • Omit VariablesInteraction

    Your interaction piece — stays live once threshold is online.

  • Paradox ShaperEnabler

    Mills and loots — gets threshold going early.

  • Uldaros TheorixBomb

    The college's Elder Sphinx. When cast, it exiles up to one nonland card of each type from your graveyard, copies them, and lets you cast the copies (MV ≤ 6) for free. Milling yourself is never a downside in this deck.

  • Divining DuelistUtility

    Flash modal creature — taps or untaps, plus a loot.

  • Proft, Sinister MastermindWildcard

    Threshold-gated menace — uncastable until seven cards are in your yard, which is exactly what this deck wants.

Stingerquill

Konstrari

  • Aerid KonstrariBomb

  • Tenured TethermageEnabler

  • Kiora of Fire and AshesWildcard

Vigorbloom

Lifegain payoffs

  • Kwia VigorbloomBomb

    The namesake Elder Sphinx — a 6/6 flying, vigilance, lifelink flier with ward {2}, and every lifegain (once per turn) makes a Lotus token that taps for three mana of any color. Protects itself and accelerates.

  • BloombrutePayoff

Manafixing

  • Rare Dual slow lands — enter tapped. Fine in a 17-land sealed deck, but expect to be a turn behind.
  • Common Fast lands — enter untapped if you control a planeswalker. Usually your best fixing — and note the planeswalkers below turn them on.
  • Hexhaven Battalion — splash enabler; fixes mana for an off-color bomb.
  • Murmuring Volume — common Book artifact: any-color mana plus a discard-fueled loot. Splash enabler.

Bombs

If you open these, build around them — even off-color, they're usually worth the fixing.

Elder Sphinxes — one per college

The five mythic Elder Sphinxes are the college namesakes themselves. Opening one is the loudest signal your pool gives: its college is your deck. All five fly.

Denzilore Fatehold

{1}{W}{U}{U} · 3/4

Flash; Flying.
Whenever you scry or surveil, put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control.

A flash flier that pumps the entire team every time you scry or surveil. Fatehold scries constantly, so it snowballs fast — and being flash, it doubles as a combat trick. Pure win condition.

Uldaros Theorix

{3}{U}{B}{B} · 5/5

Flying.
When this enters, if you cast him, exile up to one target nonland card of each card type from your graveyard. Copy those cards. You may cast any number of spells with total mana value 6 or less from among the copies without paying their mana costs.

The self-mill college literally feeds its own bomb: a loaded graveyard becomes a free hand of spells the moment he lands. Milling yourself is never a downside in UB.

Ingris Stingerquill

{B}{R}{R} · 1/4

Flying.
Whenever a creature you control attacks, that creature deals 1 damage to each opponent.
{4}: Create a 2/2 colorless Wizard Soldier creature token named Cadet. Then creatures you control gain haste until end of turn.

A three-mana bomb. Every attack is free damage to both opponents, and the {4} activation turns everything sideways — it even makes Cadets, bridging into RW aggro.

Aerid Konstrari

{1}{R}{G}{G} · 5/4

Flying.
When Aerid Konstrari enters or dies, create a Heartwood token. (It's a red and green artifact with "{T}: Add {R} or {G}.")
{6}: Create a Heartwood token. Then Aerid Konstrari gets +X/+0 until end of turn, where X is the number of artifacts you control.

Owns the Heartwood theme: ramps, fixes colors, pads the artifact count, and threatens to grow enormous. Value even when it dies.

Kwia Vigorbloom

{3}{G}{W}{W} · 6/6

Flying, vigilance, lifelink, ward {2}.
Whenever you gain life, create a colorless artifact token named Lotus with "{T}, Sacrifice this token: Add three mana of any one color." This ability triggers only once each turn.

The biggest stat monster of the five — self-protecting with ward, and it converts the lifegain plan into explosive mana. Gaining life was never this dangerous.

Planeswalkers — two versions of each character

Four planeswalker characters ship as two mythic versions each, one per archetype. Any planeswalker also turns on your common fast lands, and the "Way of the X" enchantments amplify them.

Ajani

Lifegain & tokens

Ajani Resolute {1}{W} · mythic

Static — whenever you gain life, put a loyalty counter on Ajani.
+0: gain 1 life.
−4: create a Cat Soldier token named Ajani's Pridemate with "whenever you gain life, put a +1/+1 counter on it."
−10: emblem — creatures you control get +2/+2.

The Vigorbloom pick. Gains life, feeds itself loyalty, and its −4 makes a lifegain engine of its own.

Ajani Unrelenting {4}{R}{R} · mythic

Static — whenever you activate a loyalty ability, create a Cadet token.
+1: creatures get +1/+0 and haste.
−2: discard your hand, then draw a card for each creature you control.
−3: deals 4 damage to each creature except tokens you control.

The Cadet aggro pick. Every activation widens the board, and the −3 wipes all their creatures while sparing your token army.

Chandra

Spells & card flow

Chandra, Chill of Compliance {1}{U}{U} · mythic

+1: Surveil 1; if you milled a noncreature, nonland card, put it in your hand.
+1: Add {U} (noncreature spells only).
−X: tap target creature or artifact, put X stun counters on it.
−6: emblem — whenever you cast a spell, draw a card.

Cheap and flexible — fits UB Theorix (surveil feeds threshold, and she digs milled cards back out) and UR spellslinger alike.

Chandra, Torch of Defiance {2}{R}{R} · mythic

+1: exile the top card — cast it, or she deals 2 damage to each opponent.
+1: Add {R}{R}.
−3: 4 damage to target creature.
−7: emblem — whenever you cast a spell, this deals 5 damage to any target.

The aggressive red pick — hits the Stingerquill damage plan directly and its +1 synergizes with ping payoffs.

Garruk

Big creatures

Garruk, Curse Breaker {3}{G}{G} · mythic

Static — whenever a creature you control with power 4+ enters, draw a card.
+2: untap up to two lands.
−3: create a 4/4 trample Beast token.
−4: until your next turn, attacking creatures get +2/+2 and trample.

The Konstrari ramp pick — untapping two lands is ramp, and the Konstrari plan naturally fields 4+ power creatures to draw with.

Garruk, Veiled Butcher {3}{B}{B} · mythic

Static — if a creature an opponent controls would die, exile it instead.
+2: target creature gets −4/−1 until your next turn.
−2: each player sacrifices a creature; if you sacrificed one, create a 4/4 trample Beast.
−3: each opponent discards two cards; you draw for each who didn't discard two nonland cards.

The graveyard/sac pick. Note his static exiles opponents' creatures instead of letting them die — it denies their death triggers while your own creatures still die on your terms for value.

Jace

Loyalty matters

Jace, Reality Sculptor {3}{U}{U} · rare

+1: Empower Jace X, where X is the number of Islands you control.
−3: until your next turn, attacking creatures get −5/−0.
0: exile all but the bottom card of each opponent's library — only if there are 25+ loyalty counters among Jaces you control.

The only rare of the group, so expect to see it more often. Its +1 scales with blue mana — great with fast lands and flood — and stacks toward the ultimate with any other Jace.

The Theorist, Jace Beleren {2}{U}{U} · mythic

Static — at the beginning of each opponent's draw step, you draw a card.
+1: create a 1/1 Illusion token.
−2: for each opponent, return up to one target artifact or creature they control to its hand.
−6: draw three cards, then put X +1/+1 counters on each creature you control (X = cards in hand).

Pure card advantage — the static alone out-draws most sealed decks. Fits WU Fatehold and UB Theorix, and each Illusion token is a turn of loyalty padding.

Monocolor bombs

  • Guiding Hydra — {X}{W} · rare — enters with X +1/+1 counters, and each combat you can move a counter off it to put one on every other creature you control. A commander for go-wide decks.
  • Lyra, Tolarian Archangel — {1}{U}{U} · rare — flying legend that makes a 3/3 flying Angel each turn you drew three or more cards, plus a repeatable draw-two mode. Fits WU Fatehold's card-advantage plan.
  • Liliana the Repentant — {1}{B} · rare — mills two whenever another creature or planeswalker enters, and has an exhaust ability to reanimate from your graveyard. Ideal in UB mill / BG recursion.
  • Draconic Visitor — {3}{R}{R} · rare — a 5/5 flier that upgrades every artifact token you'd create into a 5/5 flying Dragon instead. Absurd in RG Konstrari, where Heartwood and Lotus tokens are everywhere.
  • Verdant Kraken — {4}{G}{G}{G} · rare — every player's upkeep makes you a 3/3 Forest Tentacle land creature. Ramp, board presence and finisher in one.

The "Way of the X" Enchantment Cycle

All ten are uncommon Legendary Enchantments. Each one does two things when it enters: empower Jace — put loyalty counters on a Jace planeswalker token you control, first creating one (blue, with [−1]: Surveil 1 and [−3]: Draw a card) if you don't have one — and grant every planeswalker you control a bonus ability. They're sorcery-speed value that scales with the planeswalker section above.

Way of the Pyromancer {1}{R}

Planeswalkers get [+1]: Add {R}. Cheap, and it turns every walker into a mana engine — feeds the big {6}/{8} activations.

Way of the Necromancer {1}{B}

Whenever a creature you control dies, put a loyalty counter on each planeswalker. The BG graveyard archetype is a loyalty fountain.

Way of the Cryomancer {2}{U}

Planeswalkers get [−3]: copy the next instant or sorcery you cast this turn. Spellslinger value — fits UR prowess.

Way of the Deathbringer {2}{B}

Planeswalkers get [−2]: sacrifice a creature → 4/4 trample Beast. Turns your dying-matters deck into Walker loyalty.

Way of the Mentor {2}{W}

Whenever you gain life, put a loyalty counter on each planeswalker. The Vigorbloom partner — Kwia + Ajani + this is a loop.

Way of the Warlord {2}{R}

Planeswalkers get [−4]: deal 2 damage to a creature or planeswalker and 2 to a player. Extra reach for the ping decks.

Way of the Paradox {2}{G}

On each loyalty activation: gain 1 life and may play an extra land. Landfall and ramp fuel in one enchantment.

Way of the Healer {3}{W}

Planeswalkers get [−2]: create a Cadet token and surveil 1. Go-wide support with card selection.

Way of the Mind Sculptor {4}{U}

Draw a card whenever you activate a loyalty ability that removed two or more counters. Loyalty spend becomes card advantage.

Way of the Wildspeaker {4}{G}

Planeswalkers get [−4]: create a 4/4 trample Beast. The most expensive of the cycle — best in green midrange that reaches it repeatedly.
